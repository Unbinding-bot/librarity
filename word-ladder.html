How to Play

Objective

Transform the start word into the target word by changing one letter at a time. Each intermediate step must be a valid word!

Rules

  • Change exactly one letter per step.
  • Each word must be a valid English word.
  • You cannot skip steps or rearrange letters.
  • Try to reach the target in the minimum number of steps.

Example

Start: COLD
Step 1: CORD (changed L to R)
Step 2: CARD (changed O to A)
Step 3: WARD (changed C to W)
Target: WARM (changed D to M)

Scoring

  • Perfect: Reach target in minimum steps
  • Good: 1-2 extra steps
  • Okay: 3-5 extra steps
  • Using hints will add to your final step count

Features

  • 💡 Hint: Get a suggestion for the next word
  • ↶ Undo: Remove your last step
  • 🔄 Reset: Start the puzzle over
