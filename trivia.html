How to Play

Objective

Answer trivia questions correctly to earn points and build your streak!

Scoring

  • Correct Answer: Base points + bonus for difficulty and streak
  • Easy: 10 points
  • Medium: 20 points
  • Hard: 30 points
  • Streak Bonus: +5 points per consecutive correct answer

Features

  • Choose from multiple categories
  • Select difficulty level (Easy, Medium, Hard, Mixed)
  • Customize quiz length (5-20 questions)
  • Build a streak for bonus points
  • Review all answers at the end

Tips

  • Read questions carefully before answering
  • Build a streak for maximum points
  • Try different categories to expand your knowledge
  • Challenge yourself with harder difficulties
