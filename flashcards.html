How to Use Flashcards

Studying

Click on a flashcard to flip it and see the answer. Rate how well you knew the answer:

  • Hard (😓): You didn't know it. Card will appear more frequently.
  • Good (😊): You knew it after thinking. Normal review interval.
  • Easy (😎): You knew it instantly. Longer review interval.

Card Status

  • New: Cards you haven't studied yet (blue badge)
  • Learning: Cards you're currently reviewing (yellow badge)
  • Mastered: Cards you know well (green badge)

Creating Custom Decks

Click "Create Custom Deck" to make your own flashcards. Add as many cards as you need, then save the deck for later study.

Tips

  • Study regularly for best retention
  • Be honest with your ratings
  • Focus on cards you find difficult
  • Use the shuffle option for variety
