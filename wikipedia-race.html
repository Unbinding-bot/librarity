How to Play

Objective

Navigate from the starting Wikipedia article to the target article by clicking only on links within the articles.

Rules

  • You can only click on links within article content
  • Try to reach the target in as few clicks as possible
  • Time and clicks are recorded
  • You can use hints, but they add to your final count

Tips

  • Think about common topics that might connect the articles
  • Look for geographical, historical, or thematic links
  • The breadcrumb trail shows your path
  • Use the "Show Links" button to see all available links
  • Hints suggest intermediate articles

Scoring

Your score is based on the number of clicks and time taken. Fewer clicks and less time result in better scores!
